# 项目章程

Problem: manually opening and saving many Douyin shares is slow and often produces watermarked assets. Objective: a local-first Fabushi MiniApp for authorized batch resolution/download plus marketplace installation. Stakeholders are the requesting user, Fabushi product engineering, marketplace reviewers, and content rights holders. Constraints include changing Douyin endpoints, rate limits, authenticated-message access, local-disk safety, protected-main governance, and mandatory packaged E2E/Release evidence. Deliverables are MiniApp runtime/UI/package, marketplace registration, CI/E2E workflow, local download manifest, and durable evidence.

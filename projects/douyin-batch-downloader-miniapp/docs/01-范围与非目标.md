# 范围与非目标

In scope: Douyin share/video URLs already available to the user; batch parsing; watermark-free playback URL selection when the platform exposes one; retries, delay, deduplication, safe filenames, partial-failure reporting, local manifest, market search/install, and the “小李子” batch.

Out of scope: password/OTP collection, CAPTCHA automation, private API credential extraction, cookie publication, DRM circumvention, deleted/private videos without authorization, bulk crawling unrelated accounts, reposting, monetization, or local app builds/tests on this low-disk machine.

Deferred: creator-page/collection crawling and mobile-native background downloads. Owner: product owner. Revisit when explicitly requested and security-reviewed.

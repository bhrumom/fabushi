## Corrected root cause after the explicit wait

The new 30-second wait still timed out, proving the remaining failure was not surface propagation latency. Source inspection established that the controller was querying Playwright placeholder text instead of the App Surface semantic accessible name:

- `DomAppSurface.elementName()` prefers the associated `<label>` text before `placeholder`.
- The channel dialog renders `<label><span>名称</span><input ... placeholder="频道名称" /></label>` and `<label><span>描述</span><textarea ... placeholder="频道简介" /></label>`.
- Therefore the App Surface textbox names are `名称` and `描述`; `频道名称` / `频道简介` are only placeholders and cannot be used as `name` selectors while an associated label is present.

The generation race itself is now demonstrably recovered in production. The active controller blocker is the narrow semantic-selector mismatch immediately after that recovered action.
